Multi-lingual Bidirectional Sign Language Translation System for Real-Time Communication Between Hearing and Hearing-Impaired Communities in Sri Lanka Ongoing

It is bidirectional communication with Sri Lankan Languages (Tamil, Sinhala, and English) alongside Sri Lankan Sign Language (SLSL). The system works in voice-to-sign language conversion and sign-to-voice language conversion with any of the three languages to SLSL in real-time. This initiative will fundamentally help break the communication barrier between the hearing-abled society and the hearing-impaired society.
